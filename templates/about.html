THE IDEA

A little more clarity,
every day.

The Daily Brief is a small student project built to make reading the news feel more manageable for UPSC aspirants.

01

Collect

Press “Refresh news” to fetch the latest available stories from The Hindu and The Indian Express RSS feeds.

02

Understand

Each story includes a short extractive summary and a broad topic label to help you organise your reading.

03

Connect

Think about the issue, its impact and possible policy responses. Follow the source link for the complete reporting.

A note on summaries

Summaries are generated from the publishers’ feed descriptions using a simple sentence-selection method. They are not expert analysis, and topic labels are assigned by basic keyword matching. Use them as revision prompts, not as a substitute for the original article or your own notes.
